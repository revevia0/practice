# Submission note

I built a single responsive landing page because the brief makes that sufficient, and visitors need a clear path from understanding the value to starting a conversation. The page moves from the core proposition to the human-review model, then separates complete workflows from standalone tools. An interactive purchase-order example makes the automation concrete. Its details are marked as sample data; I have not claimed customer outcomes or that every listed product is already available.

The most important design decision was to put human judgement at the centre of the story. The visual flow ends with professional review because Endloops prepares the work while people make the final decision. That principle also shaped the visual language: dark navy for clarity and trust, green and teal anchored by the supplied logo, generous space, crisp typography and restrained motion. I used the logo unchanged.

With more time, I would test the wording with accountants and auditors, add product-specific pages once availability is confirmed, connect the demo request to Endloops' approved contact channel, and conduct a full in-browser accessibility and performance review.
